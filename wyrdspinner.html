Wyrdspinner is an AI-powered interactive fiction app that puts you inside a living narrative. Characters remember what you did three chapters ago. Worlds evolve based on the choices you make. No two playthroughs are the same.

Coming to iOS

What it is

An interactive story, generated as you read.

Most interactive fiction has been the same trick for forty years: branching trees of pre-written paragraphs that authors had to map out in advance. Wyrdspinner is different. The narrative is generated on the fly, in response to your choices, by Google's Gemini API.

That means the story can adapt in ways branching trees simply can’t. A character you helped in chapter one might recognize you in chapter five. A choice you made offhandedly might shape the world weeks later. The story remembers because it was never written in advance — it’s being written now, with you.

You bring the campaign. Wyrdspinner brings the world.

How it works

01

Characters that remember.

The cast keeps track of what you said, what you did, and how you treated them. Trust is earned. So is suspicion.

02

Worlds that evolve.

The world reacts to your choices over the long arc of the story — not just the next paragraph. Decisions land.

03

A look that fits the tale.

Switch themes on the fly. Read your story as parchment and quill, neon and circuitry, or whatever vibe you’re in.

Pick a world

Generated at every level

Empty save slots pull a fresh trio of campaigns out of the engine — any genre, any era, any tone. Pick one and Session Zero opens, asking who’s with you, who you are, what the story’s mood will be. Every option you see was written for the world you just chose. Don’t like the trio? Roll three more. Want to start from a blank page? “Build Your Own” walks you through it.

The choice moment

Read. Choose. Continue.

Each scene ends with a handful of choices — or the chance to write your own. Wyrdspinner weaves your decision into the next passage and keeps moving. The story flows like a novel; it just happens to be one where you’re holding the pen too.

Make it yours

One engine, many moods

Some stories want candlelight. Others want neon. Wyrdspinner ships with a library of crafted themes — pick the one that fits the tale, or roll your own.
