Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 30, 2026  ·  Sqrrlbrain Studio LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how Sqrrlbrain Studio LLC ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and handles information in connection with Inkwell, our AI-powered interactive story application.

Information We Collect

When you use Inkwell, we may collect the following:

  • Account information — email address and authentication credentials used to create and access your account.
  • Story data — your choices, story progress, saved adventures, and any custom themes or settings you create within the app.
  • Usage data — general information about how you interact with the app, such as session duration and feature usage, used solely to improve the experience.

AI-Generated Content

Inkwell uses Google's Gemini API to generate story narrative in response to your choices. Story prompts and context are transmitted to Google's API to produce this content. We do not use your story data to train any AI models. Google's handling of API data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Inkwell app
  • To save and restore your story progress across sessions
  • To respond to support requests or questions

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

Account and story data is stored securely. We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Inkwell is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
