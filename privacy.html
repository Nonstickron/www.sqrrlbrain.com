Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2026  ·  Sqrrlbrain Studio LLC

Sqrrlbrain Studio LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website at sqrrlbrain.com and develops software products including Inkwell. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information across the site and our apps.

Information collected on this website

The Sqrrlbrain Studio website is a static informational site. We do not set marketing or analytics cookies, and the site does not require an account to view. The only personal information we receive from the website is what you choose to send us by email when you contact us at the address listed in this policy. Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) may be retained briefly by our hosting provider for operational and security purposes; we do not access or use them for any other purpose.

Information collected through our apps

Inkwell

When you use Inkwell, we may collect the following:

  • Account information — email address and authentication credentials used to create and access your account.
  • Story data — your choices, story progress, saved adventures, and any custom themes or settings you create within the app.
  • Usage data — general information about how you interact with the app, such as session duration and feature usage, used solely to improve the experience.

AI-generated content. Inkwell uses Google's Gemini API to generate story narrative in response to your choices. Story prompts and context are transmitted to Google's API to produce this content. We do not use your story data to train any AI models. Google's handling of API data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Other apps. Additional Sqrrlbrain Studio apps will be added to this policy as they ship, with their own subsection describing what they collect and how it is used.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our website and apps
  • To save and restore your data within the apps where applicable
  • To respond to support requests or questions you send us

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data storage and security

Account and app data is stored securely. We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the effective date above. Continued use of our website or apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
