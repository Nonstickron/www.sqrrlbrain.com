What We Do · Sqrrlbrain Studio

Design that
ships.

Four core disciplines, one production-minded perspective. Work built to survive contact with the printer, the retailer, and the real world.

01

Packaging
Design

Overview

Retail packaging built from the dieline outward. Whether you need a folding carton, corrugated shipper, POP display, or specialty die-cut structure, the design process starts with the physical constraints — dimensions, material, fold sequence, print method — and works forward from there. The result: packaging that actually functions at production scale.

What's Included

  • Structural dieline development
  • Front, side, and base panel graphics
  • Material and print method consultation
  • Bleed, fold, and live-area accuracy
  • Press-ready files (PDF, AI, EPS)
  • Die template coordination

02

Brand
Identity

Overview

Logo systems and brand guidelines built to hold up across every application. A brand identity should work on a business card, a van, and a 12-foot banner without losing resolution, legibility, or character. Every mark is designed with reproduction in mind — spot color, full color, single-color, and reverse — before the presentation deck is even opened.

What's Included

  • Primary logo mark + wordmark
  • Color system (full, spot, 1-color, reverse)
  • Typography selection and pairing
  • Brand guidelines document
  • File package (vector + raster formats)
  • Application mockups

03

Art
Direction

Overview

Campaign concepting and creative direction for print, product marketing, and point-of-sale. Art direction means making decisions — what goes where, what gets cut, what the visual hierarchy actually communicates to a person with four seconds at a retail fixture. If your project needs a strong creative lead who can also finish the execution, that's the work.

What's Included

  • Campaign concept development
  • Layout and visual hierarchy design
  • Print and digital collateral
  • POS and retail fixture design
  • Asset direction and curation
  • Production-ready deliverables

04

Print
Production

Overview

Prepress review, file preparation, and production management built on 25 years inside a commercial print environment. This is the work most designers don't know how to do — or don't know they're doing wrong. Bringing an experienced prepress eye to your existing files catches errors before they become press mistakes, costly reprints, or missed ship dates.

What's Included

  • Prepress file review and correction
  • Color profile and ink setup
  • Bleed, trim, and safe zone audit
  • Font and image resolution check
  • Wide-format file preparation
  • Print vendor coordination (as needed)

A note on scope

"Every project is handled directly by the designer — no junior staff, no offshore production, no account layer between us. You get 25 years of production knowledge applied directly to your work."
