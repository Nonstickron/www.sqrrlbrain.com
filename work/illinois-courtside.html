Illinois Basketball Courtside Kit

Structural Design Dieline Packaging

A high-end thank-you package for top-tier University of Illinois Athletics donors. The same chassis has since shipped many times under different names — it’s become the studio’s standard structural answer for collegiate season-ticket-holder boxes, with each school swapping in their own contents.

The brief

Make it look and behave like a shoebox — the closure system, the fingerholes in the lid, the unboxing rhythm. Hold a 20oz insulated tumbler, two stacked 3D coasters, a hat, and incidentals. (On the Illinois run the tumbler swapped out for a sticker sheet and bottle opener; the chassis still had to support the full set.)

The constraints

No tabs poking through slots. No adhesive. The two structural shortcuts you’d normally reach for were both off the table, which meant tolerances had to land just right for friction fit alone — the kind of margin that’s comfortable when you’re prototyping in-house and tense when you’re not.

The solution

A two-insert system, sized around the 20oz tumbler footprint. The lower insert is a raised platform under the coasters with a logo-shaped keyhole cut through it — the customer can see the items stowed in the cavity below. The upper insert holds the coasters seated at the lid opening, with a cross-base extension that runs the width of the box to lock both inserts laterally. The lid presses everything into final position when it closes.

Production

E-flute corrugated, lithographic print, soft-touch finish. The finish requirement meant production had to leave the building — we don’t do litho-label work in-house, so this ran through an overseas vendor. Prototyping cycle to and from China is the real cost of that decision: every sign-off round adds weeks of ship time before you can put hands on a sample.

After the kit

This box has been popular with DME’s collegiate clients ever since. It ships year over year for different schools, different sports, different incidentals — mostly as season-ticket-holder and booster packages. The structural answer didn’t need rethinking; the contents change, the chassis stays.
