Commission Policy

Custom work, start to finish.

A commission is a piece made specifically for you — a carving, a relief print, something cut for a particular spot. Here's how one runs, so we're on the same page before anything starts.

How it works

  1. You tell me what you have in mind through the commission inquiry.
  2. I come back with a scope, a price, and a rough timeline.
  3. You pay a deposit — that holds your spot on the bench and I begin.
  4. I share a proof or checkpoint for your approval before I finish.
  5. Once it's approved and done, the balance is due, then I ship it.

Deposit

A deposit is required to start, and it's non-refundable once work begins. It reserves your place in the queue and covers the materials and time already spent — much of which can't be reused on someone else's piece.

Commissions are final

Because a commission is made for you specifically, completed custom work can't be refunded, returned, or exchanged. This is why the proof step matters — you sign off before I take it to the finish.

Approval & revisions

We'll settle the scope up front so there are no surprises. I'll share a proof at a sensible checkpoint, and reasonable adjustments at that stage are part of the process. Changes that go beyond the agreed scope — a new direction, added elements — may add cost and time, and I'll tell you before doing them, never after.

Timeline

Any date I give is an honest estimate, not a guarantee. Handmade work moves at the speed of the material, and I'd rather it be right than rushed. I'll keep you posted if something shifts.

Rights

You own the physical piece. I keep the right to photograph the finished work and show it as part of my portfolio unless we agree otherwise in writing beforehand.

Damaged in transit

If your finished commission arrives damaged, email me at hello@sqrrlbrain.com within 7 days of delivery with a photo and I'll make it right.

Starting one

Ready, or just curious what's possible? Send me the idea through the commission inquiry and we'll go from there. Shop purchases have their own terms — see the Shop Policy.
