# Shop Pricing Research — Sept 2026

A market scan of comparable handmade / indie listings (mostly Etsy) for the current product spread, with a suggested price band for each and the reasoning. **These are anchors, not rules — you know the time each piece actually takes.**

## Principles first (these matter more than the numbers)

1. **You're on your own site, not Etsy's search feed.** Someone on sqrrlbrain.com came for *you*, not to comparison-shop the cheapest dragon. You don't have to price to the Etsy floor — price for the craft. Etsy's low end is mostly makers undervaluing their own time.
2. **Shipping is baked into your prices** (free-shipping model). Add your real pack + postage (~$5–9 by size/weight) into each price. An Etsy "$20 + free shipping" item nets the maker only ~$12–14 after shipping and Etsy's ~6.5% + fees.
3. **Your Stripe cut is smaller than Etsy's** (~2.9% + 30¢ vs Etsy's ~6.5% + listing + payment). Same sticker price = you keep more; or sit a hair under Etsy and still net more.
4. **Don't underprice the handmade work.** Materials + a real hourly rate is the floor. A two-hour carving shouldn't be priced like a 3D print that ran unattended overnight.

## Per-category ranges

### Carvings — figurines (~3–4 in, one-of-a-kind)
- **Market:** $12–80+. Simple animals $12–25; detailed/character pieces $30–80 (3" cat $21, 4" character $29, 4" bird sculpture $47).
- **Suggested for yours: $35–65.** One-of-a-kind + real carving time puts you above the simple-animal floor; detailed pieces justify $50–65+.

### 3D Prints (decor / toys / wall-art / storage)
- **Market:** $1.75–16, most $8–10. **The price-sensitive category** — everyone has a printer, so the floor is low (articulated dragon $8, T-Rex $10, fidget set $16).
- **Suggested for yours: $12–30** by size + finish. Your hand-finishing/sealing/curation is the differentiator, but don't expect carving margins here.

### Relief Prints — on paper
- **Market:** $3–28+. Mini $3–5; standard small $10–17; larger/detailed (4×6+) $18–28+.
- **Suggested for yours: $12–25** standard; **$25–40** for larger or numbered editions (signing + numbering earns the upper end).

### Relief Prints — apparel (hand block-printed tees)
- **Market:** $20–41, most $25–40; hand-carved-design tees $35–40.
- **Suggested for yours: $28–40.** Printed-to-order from your own carved block supports the upper half.

### Relief Prints — patches
- **Market:** $3.50–12+; handmade block-printed ~$12.
- **Suggested for yours: $6–15** each — or sell as sets / 3-packs to lift the order value (small item, so lean on bundles).

### Notebooks (hand-bound, by size)
- **Market:** A5/A6 from ~$12; cloth-cover $18–25; leather $21–47; traveller's ~$47.
- **Suggested (paper / cloth cover):** Pocket & A6 **$14–22** · A5 & B5 **$20–32** · A4 **$28–42**. Leather / special paper pushes higher.
