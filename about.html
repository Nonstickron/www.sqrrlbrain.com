Twenty-five years of learning
how things actually get made.

Sqrrlbrain Studio is the practice of Ronny Goodrich — a designer and developer who has spent his entire professional life inside commercial print production, not adjacent to it. He started as a graphic designer, moved into prepress, and worked up through packaging design and structural engineering — entirely self-taught, every step. The expertise here is the kind that doesn't come from design school: it comes from knowing why a folding carton fails on press, how a die-cut tolerance will ruin a retail shelf, and what "print-ready" actually means to the person running the job.

The drive is the same thing that drives the rest of his life: making things. Building things. Fixing things. He sees something he likes and his first thought is — "I bet I can make that." Once a solution is in his head, it doesn't leave him alone until it exists.

Sqrrlbrain Studio LLC was founded in 2025 as an independent practice. Every project is handled directly by Ronny — no junior designers, no offshore production, no account layer between client and designer. The structural thinking is baked in, not bolted on at the end.

The studio also builds digital products: Inkwell (an interactive fiction app preparing for TestFlight beta), SqrrledAway (household meal planning), and TRACT (print-on-demand apparel). Software is built through the same self-taught, production-minded lens as the print work — if it ships, it works.

Based in Edgewater, Florida. Remote-first. Available for packaging design, brand identity, print production, wide-format, and select software collaborations.

How We Work

01

Structure Before Graphics

Every packaging project starts with the dieline — dimensions, material, fold sequence, print constraints. Graphics that don't account for structure fail on press. We build the chassis first.

02

Production-Ready by Default

Files leave this studio press-ready. That means correct bleeds, appropriate resolution, proper spot color setup, and a working knowledge of what your printer actually needs — not what looks good on screen.

03

No Junior Handoffs

You work directly with the designer who will execute your project. There are no account managers between us, no junior designers finishing the file, and no offshore production. What you see is what you get.
