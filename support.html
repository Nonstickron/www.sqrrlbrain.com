Inkwell

For help with Inkwell — bug reports, account questions, story content concerns, or feature feedback — reach us at the same address:

hello@sqrrlbrain.com

To help us resolve issues quickly, please include:

  • The version of Inkwell you're using (Settings → About)
  • Your device and OS version (e.g. iPhone 14, iOS 18.2)
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen
  • Steps to reproduce, if you can
