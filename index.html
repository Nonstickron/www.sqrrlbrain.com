About

A studio built on craft, precision, and a stubborn love of paper.

Sqrrlbrain Studio LLC is the independent creative practice of Ronald Goodrich — a designer with over 25 years of professional experience across packaging, brand, and print. Based in Edgewater, Florida, the studio brings senior-level design thinking to projects that demand more than a template.

From complex structural packaging to complete brand systems, every project is approached with the same commitment: work that holds up under scrutiny, stands out on shelf, and means something to the people who made it.
