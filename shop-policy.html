Shop Policy

Before you buy.

Everything in the shop is made by hand, one piece at a time. A few things worth knowing before you order.

All sales are final

I don't offer refunds or exchanges for a change of mind, a gift that didn't land, fit, or because a handmade piece looks a little different from its photo. Please read the description and look closely at the photos before you order. The one exception is damaged or wrong items — see below.

Handmade means variation

These are one-of-a-kind and made-to-order pieces. Grain, color, ink coverage, and small tool marks vary from piece to piece and from the photos on the site. That variation is the nature of handmade work, not a flaw — it's why you're buying something made by a person and not stamped out by a machine.

Made-to-order items

Anything marked "made to order" is carved, printed, or made after you order it, so it can't be cancelled once I've started. Each listing shows an estimated lead time. Those are honest estimates, not guarantees — handmade work moves at the speed of the material, and I'll let you know if something needs more time.

Shipping

Shipping is free within the United States, and it's already included in the price. I don't ship internationally yet. Ready-made pieces usually go out within a few business days; made-to-order pieces ship after their listed lead time. You'll get tracking once it's on its way.

Damaged or wrong items

This is the one exception to "all sales final." If your order arrives damaged, or I sent the wrong thing, email me at hello@sqrrlbrain.com within 7 days of delivery with a photo, and I'll make it right — a replacement where possible, or a refund.

Questions

Ask before you buy if anything's unclear — I'd rather answer a question than have a piece go to the wrong home. Reach me at hello@sqrrlbrain.com or through the contact page. Commission work has its own terms — see the Commission Policy.
