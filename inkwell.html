What it is

An interactive story, generated as you read.

Most interactive fiction has been the same trick for forty years: branching trees of pre-written paragraphs that authors had to map out in advance. Inkwell is different. The narrative is generated on the fly, in response to your choices, by Google's Gemini API.

That means the story can adapt in ways branching trees simply can’t. A character you helped in chapter one might recognize you in chapter five. A choice you made offhandedly might shape the world weeks later. The story remembers because it was never written in advance — it’s being written now, with you.

You bring the campaign. Inkwell brings the world.

How it works

01

Characters that remember.

The cast keeps track of what you said, what you did, and how you treated them. Trust is earned. So is suspicion.

02

Worlds that evolve.

The world reacts to your choices over the long arc of the story — not just the next paragraph. Decisions land.

03

A look that fits the tale.

Switch themes on the fly. Read your story as parchment and quill, neon and circuitry, or whatever vibe you’re in.

The choice moment

Read. Choose. Continue.

Each scene ends with a handful of choices — or the chance to write your own. Inkwell weaves your decision into the next passage and keeps moving. The story flows like a novel; it just happens to be one where you’re holding the pen too.
